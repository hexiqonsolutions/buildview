# Branding and visual guidelines

These rules apply to @build_view360 and to the Facebook Page cover. They use the brand already on the product. They do not introduce a new logo, a new green, or a photography-studio look.

No files in this pack were uploaded to Instagram.

## Source assets

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| `db-logo.png` in the site brand, wordmark in white on black | Cover images, carousel end frames, anywhere the ground is `#050505` |
| `wb-logo.png`, dark wordmark | Only on a white or `#f8fafc` ground. Do not place it on black. It disappears |
| Current Instagram avatar, the mark on a lime circle | Profile photo. Already recognisable. Do not redraw it for launch |
| Live site | The reference for tone: black hero, white Poppins headlines, lime used in small quantities |

The wordmark is lowercase “buildview” with the folded play-mark. Do not set the name in a different typeface, do not add a tagline inside the logo, and do not put the mark on a photograph.

## Colour

From `src/app/globals.css`.

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary ground | `#050505` | Feed backgrounds, Reel covers, story backgrounds |
| Elevated ground | `#1a1a1a` | A second panel inside a carousel, not a new brand colour |
| Accent | `#A4CF30` | One rule, one word, or one button per frame |
| Accent, darker | `#76B82D` | Small text on a light ground, if you ever post a light frame |
| Light ground | `#F8FAFC` | Optional light carousel frame. Most of the grid should stay dark so the profile matches the website hero |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Headlines and body on dark |
| Muted text | `#CBD5E1` | Secondary lines. Do not go greyer than this on black |

Lime is a signal, not a background. The current profile photo is the exception, because a tiny avatar needs the mark to read. Do not fill feed posts with lime.

Do not use the interior-ad palette visible on the other account: red banners, gold price strips, or furniture photography.

## Type

The product uses Poppins for headlines and Open Sans for body. Use the same pair in Canva, Figma, or Photoshop.

| Role | Face | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | Poppins | 600 or 700 | Sentence case or title case. Not all-caps paragraphs |
| Eyebrow | Poppins | 600 | 11–13 px equivalent, tracking open, colour `#A4CF30` |
| Body | Open Sans | 400 or 500 | Short lines. 6 to 10 words a line on a phone |
| Numbers | Poppins | 600 | Only real numbers, such as a date. No fake percentages |

Do not use a condensed “construction stencil” font, a script, or a serif.

## Layout

Feed still and carousel: **1080 × 1350** (4:5). The profile grid crops toward the centre, so the logo and the headline stay inside the middle square. Leave 80 px of padding from the edges of that square.

Reel and story: **1080 × 1920**. Keep text out of the top 250 px and the bottom 320 px. Those areas are covered by the username, the caption, and the reply field. The cover title must also sit in the centre square, because the grid shows the cover as a square crop.

Carousel length: 5 to 7 frames. Frame 1 is the headline. The last frame is the CTA and the logo. Do not put the URL in a size that looks like a footnote. If the URL is on the image, set it in Open Sans at a size you can read on a phone, or leave the URL to the caption only. The calendar’s end frames can say “Book a demo” without the full URL on the image. The caption always carries the URL.

Logo position: top-left of a carousel, small, or centred on the end frame. Not both at full size.

One idea per frame. If a frame needs a paragraph, it is two frames.

## Profile branding

Once the approved bio is live, the profile should read in this order:

1. Avatar: existing mark.
2. Name: `BuildView | Construction Tech`.
3. Bio A.
4. Website button to `/links`.
5. Highlights, in the order below.
6. The nine-post grid.

That stack is what makes a new account look established. Consistency does it, not a follower count.

## Nine-post launch grid

Posted in the calendar as Days 1, 3, 5, 8, 10, 12, 15, 17 and 19. On the phone they appear in this visual order, newest at the top left, so **after all nine are live the top-left post is “Book a demo”** and the bottom-right is “What BuildView is”.

Instagram shows the newest post at the top left. Publish in this order, oldest first:

1. What BuildView is
2. The update problem
3. 360° tours
4. Compare progress
5. Reports and timeline
6. Documents
7. Issues
8. Who it is for
9. Book a demo

The finished grid then reads:

| | Left | Centre | Right |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top | 9 Book a demo | 8 Who it is for | 7 Issues |
| Middle | 6 Documents | 5 Reports and timeline | 4 Compare progress |
| Bottom | 3 360° tours | 2 The update problem | 1 What BuildView is |

A visitor’s eye hits the demo first, then the audience, then the modules, and the definition anchors the bottom right. That is the intended order. Do not rearrange after posting.

Each cover uses the same skeleton: black ground, lime eyebrow with the series label “BuildView”, white headline from the calendar, small mark. If the covers match, the grid looks designed even before anyone taps.

## Reel covers

Template, 1080 × 1920, with the title also safe inside the centre 1080 × 1080:

- Ground `#050505`.
- Eyebrow, lime: `BUILDVIEW` in small tracked capitals. This is the only all-caps element.
- Title, Poppins, white, two lines maximum, from the calendar cover line.
- A 4 px lime rule under the title, 64 px wide. Not a full-width bar.
- Mark at the bottom of the safe square, small.
- No screenshots on the cover. The motion is inside the Reel. The cover is the index.

## Story template

- Ground `#050505`.
- Top safe area left empty.
- Eyebrow “BuildView” in lime, then one sentence in Poppins.
- If the story is a poll or question, the sticker sits below the sentence, not over it.
- End a sequence with a button that matches the site: `#A4CF30` ground, `#050505` text, Poppins, label “Book a demo”.
- Do not use Instagram’s default neon stickers, GIFs, or the interior creative.

## Highlight covers

Export 1080 × 1080, then Instagram will circle-crop. Draw inside the centre circle, with the icon not touching the edge.

Ground `#050505`. Icon in `#A4CF30`, simple line icon, 2 px. No words on the cover. The highlight title carries the words. A word on a circular crop will not be readable.

| Highlight title | Icon idea | Why this title |
|---|---|---|
| About | The mark, tiny | Brand story and the Thane base |
| Features | Four small squares | The module index |
| How It Works | Four dots in a row | Capture, monitor, review, deliver |
| 360 Tours | A circle with a play-shaped cut, echoing the mark. Do not redraw the logo as a new symbol | The visual product |
| Projects | A simple building outline | Scenarios, not client jobs |
| Benefits | An eye, or a square with a check | Visibility and a shared record. Not a trophy |
| FAQ | A question mark | Objection handling |
| Contact | An arrow | The demo |

Title case as written. Instagram highlight titles are short; these fit.

## Highlight content

Save the stories below from the calendar, plus these frames if you need the highlight to make sense before that day arrives. Three to six frames each. Same template.

**About.** What BuildView is (Day 1’s first line). Based in Thane. Works with project teams in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane. Not a photography or interior page (Day 6).

**Features.** One frame each: 360° tours, compare progress, reports, timeline, documents, issues, client dashboard. Use the sentences from `01-product-positioning.md`. No extra modules.

**How It Works.** The four stages from the website. Capture: record the site with a 360° camera and publish the tour. Monitor: reports, issues, documents, timeline. Review: stakeholders open the project remotely, within their role. Deliver: the record stays organised. Do not imply the software captures the scan by itself.

**360 Tours.** What a tour is. It lives on the project by date. Compare is a separate frame: two visits, side by side. Limit frame: not a live CCTV feed.

**Projects.** The six scenario types from the live projects page, with that page’s caution: these are situations, not case studies. Final frame: “A live example is part of the demo, when we are allowed to show it.”

**Benefits.** Only benefits the product can support: fewer scattered updates, a dated visual record, one place for files and issues, access by role, a stakeholder can review without travelling. Do not write time saved, cost saved, or “transparency increased by”.

**FAQ.** Use the answers in `10-lead-generation-automation.md`. Six frames is enough.

**Contact.** Email, sales phone, Thane address, and “Book a demo” to `/contact`. Add WhatsApp only after the number is confirmed.

## Carousel system

Every feature carousel:

1. Eyebrow: the pillar, for example “Platform”.
2. Headline.
3. One supporting sentence.
4. Two or three frames of substance.
5. One frame of limit, when the topic is easy to over-claim (tours, compare, reports).
6. Close: “Book a demo” and the mark.

Repeat the skeleton so a buyer who sees three posts recognises the company before they read the name.

## Words

Use: construction monitoring, project record, site tour, stakeholder, role, timeline, issue, drawing, demo.

Do not use: disruptive, seamless, next-gen, empower, leverage, “excited to”, “DM for prices”, “tag a friend”, BHK, interior, turnkey home.

The company’s line can be repeated: “See every construction project. Make every decision faster.” It is already public. Do not attribute it to a client.

## Accessibility

- White on `#050505` for body text. Lime only for short labels.
- Do not put lime text on white. Use `#76B82D` if the ground is light.
- Describe the image in the alt text supplied in the calendar.
- Burn captions into Reels. Many people watch without sound. The calendar does not depend on a voiceover.
- Do not convey status by colour alone. An issue status, if you ever show one, also has the word.
