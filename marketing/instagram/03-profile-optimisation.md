# Profile optimisation — @build_view360

Nothing below has been applied. Paste it only after you approve the canonical account in `02-instagram-account-audit.md`.

Character limits used: display name 30, bio 150. Counts were checked against the exact strings in this file.

## Username

**Keep `build_view360` for launch.**

Purpose: the account is new, the handle is understandable, and a rename now creates a second thing to approve before the profile can even explain the product. Instagram also limits how often a username can change.

Check once, in Edit profile, whether `buildview360` (no underscore) is free. If it is free, it is easier to say in a meeting and easier to type. Change it only with your approval, and only before the website and printed material start using the underscore. Do not try to take `buildview.360` while the other account exists.

Do not use a photography, interior, or “quotes” username.

## Display name

**Use:** `BuildView | Construction Tech`

Count: 29 characters.

Purpose: the name field is searchable. “Construction Tech” is the shortest accurate description that still fits, and it matches the wording already prepared in the product config, without the typo on the other account.

Alternates if you want a different emphasis, still inside the limit:

| Name | Characters | Use if |
|---|---|---|
| `BuildView \| Construction Tech` | 29 | Default. Best balance of brand and category |
| `BuildView \| Site Monitoring` | 27 | You prefer “monitoring” over “tech” in search |
| `BuildView \| 360 Monitoring` | 26 | You want the visual product in the name |

Do not use `Buildview` alone. That is the current name, and it does not tell a buyer what you sell.

## Category

**Software Company.**

Purpose: BuildView is a platform. “Construction Company” or “Photographer” would attract the wrong enquiries and would fight the positioning. If Software Company is not offered in your region’s list, use the closest technology or software label, not interior design, real estate, or photography.

## Action buttons

On a professional account: **Edit profile → Action buttons** (Instagram sometimes labels this Contact options).

Set, in this order:

1. **Email** — buildviewsales@gmail.com. Works even before WhatsApp is confirmed.
2. **Call** — sales number above.
3. **WhatsApp** — only after you confirm the Business account. Prefill the message in `10-lead-generation-automation.md`.
4. **Book** — only if Instagram offers a booking button that can open your existing contact scheduler. If the only booking option is a third-party tool you do not use, skip it. The website button already opens the demo path.

Purpose: a profile visitor who will not read a caption can still request a demo in one tap. The primary commercial action remains the website button, because the form and scheduler qualify the enquiry. WhatsApp is the fast secondary path for people who already know they want to talk.

## Professional account and business information

Do this while logged into @build_view360:

1. Profile → menu → **Settings and activity** → **Account type and tools** → **Switch to professional account**.
2. Choose **Business**, not Creator. A business account can connect to a Facebook Page, Meta advertising, and action buttons. Creator is the wrong container for a B2B demo funnel.
3. Category: **Software Company**.
4. If asked to connect a Facebook Page, stop and follow `05-meta-business-setup.md` if the Page does not exist yet. Connecting the wrong personal Page is harder to undo than waiting a day.
5. Turn on professional dashboard tools: insights, messages, and contacts. You need these for the inbox and the lead replies.

Suggested contact options inside the professional dashboard, once the buttons exist:

- Email and phone as above.
- Address: the Thane office.

## What not to put on the profile

- “India’s leading”, client counts, or “trusted by” names.
- Interior design, BHK, photography packages, or “get a quote today” in the style of the other account’s visible post.
- A list of features that are classified as incomplete or out of bounds in `01-product-positioning.md`.
- The support phone as the main button.
- A link to @buildview.360, unless you have decided that account is canonical and this new one will not be used.
