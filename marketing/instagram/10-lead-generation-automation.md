# Lead generation and automation

Voice: a competent person at BuildView, writing in short sentences. No emoji, no “Hey superstar”, no pretence that a meeting is already on the calendar. The contact form promises a reply within one business day. These messages keep that promise.

Automation may send the first acknowledgement. A person sends everything that qualifies, books, or refuses.

Do not install these until you approve the wording and the canonical Instagram account. The product does not contain a CRM. Do not tell a buyer you logged them in a system you do not have.

## Instant Instagram reply

Send once, the first time someone messages the professional account.

> Thanks for writing to BuildView. This is the construction monitoring platform — 360° site tours, progress records, documents and issue tracking for project teams.
>
> Tell us your name, company and the type of project, and a person from the team will reply within one business day. If you would rather book directly, use the demo form on our profile.

Purpose: they know they reached the right company, they know a person is coming, and they are given the form if they are ready.

## Frequently asked questions

Use these in highlights, story replies, and saved replies. They match the public site. Where the site is silent, the answer says so.

**What is BuildView?**
A construction monitoring and documentation platform. Teams keep 360° site tours, progress reports, documents, a timeline and issues on one project, and each person sees what their role allows.

**Is this a 360° photography service?**
Capture is part of how a tour gets onto the project. The thing we are here to talk about is the platform: remote review, records and access. We are not an interior studio and we do not sell BHK packages.

**How quickly can a project be set up?**
The public answer is accurate: most projects can be onboarded within a few days, depending on size and the documents required. Do not promise “same day” in a DM.

**Can you handle more than one project?**
Yes. A client can have more than one project in the portal.

**Can our client log in?**
Yes. Access is role-based. Reviewers, site staff and a client admin do not get the same actions.

**Can we upload our own reports and drawings?**
Yes. The document hub accepts drawings, BOQs, contracts, approvals, technical documents and other files. Reports can be progress, quality, inspection or safety reports. Someone on the team still has to produce the file. The platform does not write it.

**Do you integrate with our existing tools?**
There is no public integration with ERP, BIM, or accounts software to promise. The honest reply is: we will look at how you report today and tell you what fits inside BuildView, and what does not.

**How often should the site be captured?**
As often as the milestones and the reporting cycle require. BuildView stores the dated visits so the team can compare them. We do not publish a mandatory weekly quota.

**Is the tour a live camera?**
No. It is a captured 360° tour, published on the project. Compare shows two visits. It is not CCTV.

**Do you use AI to write reports?**
Do not say yes. A project screen can summarise reports and issues that are already uploaded. That is a digest of existing records, not a system that inspects the site or predicts delay.

**Where are you based?**
Lok Upvan Phase 2, Thane, Maharashtra. We speak with teams about projects in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane. Do not invent another office.

**What does it cost?**
There is no public price. Say: “Pricing depends on the projects and the way your team needs access. We cover that after we understand the scope, usually on or after the demo.” Do not invent a package.

## Qualification questions

Ask only what they have not already answered. Five is enough.

1. What is your role, and which company is asking?
2. What are you building, and in which city?
3. Is the project in progress, about to start, or already handed over?
4. Who else needs to see the project — owner, architect, PMC, contractor?
5. What do you want monitoring to replace: site visits for remote stakeholders, the progress report, the file trail, or the issue list?

A qualified lead has a role, a live or imminent project, and a company. Book the demo when those three are present. You can collect the other two on the call.

## When it is not a fit

Interior or home-decoration enquiry:

> Thank you for writing. BuildView is construction monitoring software for project teams. We are not able to help with an interior or home quote. I hope you find the right studio.

Student with no organisation:

> Thank you. The demos are for teams delivering a project. If you are researching, the public overview is here: https://buildview-tawny.vercel.app/services

One-off social reel of a site, with no monitoring need:

> Thank you. We are not a content studio. If the site needs an ongoing visual record for the project team, we can talk about the platform. If you only need a film, we are not the right people.

## Follow-up

Send from the same person who answered. Stop when they reply or when they say no.

**Day 2, if they went quiet after a useful first reply**

> Sharing this in case it was buried. If a demo is useful, send the project type and your role and we will suggest a time. The form is here if that is easier: https://buildview-tawny.vercel.app/contact

**Day 5**

> We will leave the conversation here for now. If a project needs remote visibility — tours, reports, documents or issues — write to us and we will pick it up.

Do not send a third chase. Do not offer a discount. There is no published price to discount.

**After a demo**

> Thank you for the time today. We looked at [the modules you actually showed: tours / compare / reports / documents / issues / roles].
>
> You were going to [the next step they agreed, in their words]. I will [your action] by [the day you agreed].
>
> If I have the next step wrong, reply and correct it.

If they did not agree a next step, write:

> If you want to continue, the useful next step is a list of the projects and the people who need access. We will not hold a place or invent a proposal you have not asked for.

## Where the lead lives

Today a complete lead is an email from the contact form, provided Resend is configured, plus whatever arrives in Instagram and WhatsApp. Those are three inboxes. The commercial risk is a message that nobody owns.

Until a CRM exists, use one sheet the sales owner updates the same day. Columns:

- Date
- Source: Instagram, WhatsApp, form, phone
- Name
- Company
- Email
- Phone
- Role
- Project type
- City
- Stage: new, qualified, demo booked, demo done, not a fit, closed
- Next step and date
- Owner

Do not paste passwords, tokens, or full drawing lists into that sheet.

**CRM recommendation, when you are ready to buy one.** Zoho CRM is the practical first choice for a Thane team that also wants WhatsApp in the same conversation. HubSpot’s free CRM is the alternative if you prefer the form and email tracking and you will add WhatsApp later. Either one is enough. Do not build a CRM inside the BuildView application for this launch, and do not connect Meta Lead Ads until the sheet, or the CRM, has a named owner.

A sensible later connection, not part of this setup: the contact form already sends an email. Forward that inbox into the CRM. Instagram and WhatsApp still need a person, or a supported inbox integration, to create the same record. There is no native BuildView-to-CRM sync to switch on.

## Daily routine

Each business morning, one person:

1. Checks Instagram inbox, WhatsApp, and buildviewsales@gmail.com.
2. Sends the human reply where the instant message has already gone out.
3. Adds a row to the sheet.
4. Books or declines before the end of the day.

That routine is the automation that matters. The greeting only stops the silence.
