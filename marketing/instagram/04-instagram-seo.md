# Instagram SEO

Purpose: help a developer, architect, contractor, or PMC who is already looking for construction visibility find @build_view360, understand the offer in the profile, and reach the demo page. Follower growth is a side effect. The metric that matters is a qualified enquiry.

Instagram search reads the name, username, bio, captions, alt text, on-screen text, and account activity. It does not reward a block of 30 broad hashtags. This plan uses a small set of accurate phrases, repeated in the places Instagram and a human buyer both see.

## How each surface should use the words

**Name.** `BuildView | Construction Tech` puts a category word where Instagram search looks first.

**Username.** `build_view360` already contains the brand and “360”, which supports the tour product. Do not stuff keywords into the username.

**Bio.** Bio A in `03-profile-optimisation.md` carries buyers plus the real modules. Bio C is the local variant. One bio only.

**Captions.** First two lines must make sense before “more”. Put one primary keyword in the first sentence, in normal English. Put the demo link once. Do not repeat the keyword list at the bottom in addition to hashtags.

**On-screen text.** Reel covers and carousel titles should be short enough to read on a phone. Use one keyword, not three. Example: “360° site tours” rather than “360° construction documentation and remote monitoring software”.

**Alt text.** Write it as a description of the image for someone who cannot see it, and include one natural keyword. Instagram’s manual alt text is available in Advanced settings before posting. Do not paste the caption again.

Example: “BuildView screen showing a 360 degree construction site tour beside a project timeline.”

**Location.** Tag Thane on the profile and on posts about the company. Tag a project city only when the image is of a project you are allowed to locate. Do not geotag Navi Mumbai on a graphic that has no Navi Mumbai subject.

**Hashtags.** Five to seven. Mix one brand tag, two or three product tags, and, on local posts only, one place tag. Put them at the end of the caption. Do not use `#love`, `#follow`, `#instagood`, or tags you cannot justify.

**Alt and hashtag pairing.** The caption carries the sentence. The alt text describes the picture. The hashtags are the smallest part. If a phrase is already in the first line, it does not also need to be a hashtag.

`#BuildView` is the only branded tag. It will not bring strangers at the start. It keeps your own posts grouped so a profile visitor can tap one tag and see the body of work later.

## Discoverability habits that match a B2B sale

- Post from the professional account, not a personal remix.
- Write captions in the language your buyers use in meetings: site progress, drawings, issues, stakeholders, handover. Not “content drop”.
- Show the product UI or an original diagram. A stock crane photo with keyword text will not rank as well as a clear product frame, and it looks like every other construction page.
- Answer comments and story replies in the first day. Instagram treats conversation as a sign the account is active, and a reply is also a qualification conversation.
- Do not follow hundreds of unrelated accounts to inflate the following count. The new profile’s 0 following is more professional than a follow-for-follow list.
- Keep the name, bio, and highlight titles stable for the 30 days. Changing them every week removes the phrase you are trying to get associated with the account.
- When a Reel is a screen recording, say “BuildView” and “construction” in the first spoken or on-screen line. That is the transcript Instagram can read.
- Invite teammates to add the brand name only. Do not ask them to comment with keywords.

## Search phrases to test after 30 days

Look in Instagram search, logged into the professional account, and note whether the profile appears for:

- BuildView
- construction monitoring
- construction progress
- 360 construction
- virtual site tour

Also check Professional dashboard → Insights → Accounts reached, and the website’s UTM views for `utm_campaign=buildview_profile`, `buildview_reels`, `buildview_carousel`, and `buildview_stories`.

A useful result is a demo enquiry that names a project role and a project type. A weak result is reach from interior, student, or giveaway audiences. If that traffic appears, remove the post that caused it rather than adding more broad hashtags.
